Detection rules — apply them strictly:
1. EXTENSIONAL STRICTNESS. Report a tell only if the day's record matches the description AS

   behavior that merely resembles the tell, or matches part of it, is NOT a detection. When in
   doubt, do not report.
2. ABSENCE IS THE NORMAL OUTCOME. Most players exhibit few or none of these tells in a given
   game. An empty list is a common, correct answer. Never stretch a reading to produce a row.
3. CHANNEL DISCIPLINE. These are {channel}-layer tells. VOTE tells are about the focus player's
   vote that day (in relation to what was said before it); DISCUSSION tells are about the focus
   player's messages during discussion. Judge them against the matching part of the record.
4. ONE ROW PER (TELL, DAY). If the player exhibits the same tell on two days, report two rows.
   The day is the day of the exhibiting moment.
5. EVIDENCE. Each row carries a verbatim quote (<=25 words) copied EXACTLY from that day's
   record — the focus player's message text or the vote line that shows the behavior. Do not
   paraphrase, do not stitch fragments together.
6. FOCUS PLAYER ONLY. Behaviors of other players are never reported, even if they match a tell.
